title: Find your way around
description: Know where to start work, check progress, review a request, and find a finished file.
---

Knotra Desktop keeps the plan, the work in progress, and your review requests in separate places.

## Pipelines: choose the work

A pipeline is a workflow: a saved plan with steps to follow. Open **Pipelines** to find an existing plan or choose **New pipeline** to start from a prepared example.

Use this area when you want to start a task. For your first real example, follow [Review an announcement](/docs/guides/first-workflow).

## Runs: check progress

A run is one attempt to carry out a workflow. Running the same workflow again creates another run.

Open **Runs** to see which steps have finished, which are working, and which need attention. Select a step to inspect what it received and produced.

Closing desktop does not cancel work on the engine. Reconnect to check its progress.

## Inbox: respond to a request

Open **Inbox** when a workflow needs a person's review. Read the request and its supporting information before responding.

Your response belongs to that specific run. The required response can differ between workflows.

## Artifacts: find the files

An artifact is a file saved by a workflow. Open **Artifacts** to preview or export a result.

For example, the announcement workflow produces the final announcement and a record of its reviews.

## Settings: connect and choose your language

Use **Settings** to connect to the engine address supplied by your team. You can also switch the interface between English and Russian.

## Demo or real work?

**Runs**, **Inbox**, and **Artifacts** have **Guided demo** and **Engine** tabs. Choose **Guided demo** for sample results. Choose **Engine** for work done by your connected service.
