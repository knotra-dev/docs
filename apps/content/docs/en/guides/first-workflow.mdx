Explore the example below. The real workflow creates files; it does not send the announcement to customers.

<WorkflowWalkthrough />

<details>
<summary>Run this with your team</summary>

[Connect first](/docs/getting-started). Ask which profile to use; choose `local` only if your teammate prepared it.

1. Open **Pipelines**.
2. Select **From brief to reviewed publication**.
3. Choose **Run**.
4. Select the supplied **Engine profile**.
5. Choose **Start run**.
6. Open **Inbox**.
7. Choose **Engine**.
8. Open this run's request.
9. Expand **Inputs**.
10. Review the brief, draft, and editorial review.
11. Paste `{"approved":true,"feedback":""}` into **Response output ports** to approve.
12. Choose **Submit response**.

Missing the example? Find it under **New pipeline → Ready to run**. Keep its sample brief for your first try.

After completion, export `publication.md` from **Artifacts → Engine**. Read the revised text before using it. `review-history.json` keeps the reviews.

To decline, leave the request open or use **Request cancellation** in the run. This example does not accept a false approval response.

</details>
