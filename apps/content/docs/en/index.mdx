---
title: What is Knotra?
description: Turn a repeatable task into a process you can follow, review, and use again.
---

Knotra helps you repeat a piece of work with clear steps, checks, and a place for human review.

A **workflow** is the saved plan for that work. For example, preparing a customer announcement can follow the same plan each time:

1. AI writes a draft from your brief.
2. A separate AI review compares the draft with the supplied facts.
3. You review the draft and give feedback.
4. AI revises the text.
5. Knotra saves the final document and review history as files.

You can see what happened at each step. When the process needs your response, it waits for you.

## What you do in Knotra

Open a prepared workflow, start the work, review its progress, and respond when asked. Keep the finished files for your next task.

You do not need to write code to explore the app or use a workflow that your team has already set up. A developer prepares the service that does the work and connects the required AI model.
