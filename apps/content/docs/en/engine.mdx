Desktop is your workspace. The engine is the service doing the work. Your team prepares the connection; you follow progress and respond when a step needs your judgment.

<HowItWorks />

**Workflow:** the saved plan. **Run:** one attempt to follow it. **Artifact:** a result file you keep.

Closing desktop does not cancel work on the engine. Reconnect to check its progress.
