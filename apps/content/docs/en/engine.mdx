Desktop is where you build and review. The engine runs the work and saves progress.

<MermaidDiagram title="Who does what" chart={'flowchart LR\n  D[Desktop: plan and review] --> E[Engine: run and save]\n  E --> M[Models]\n  E --> T[Tools and sandboxes]\n  M --> E\n  T --> E\n  E -->|Progress and results| D'} />

## Start the service that runs your steps

1. [Save a model source](/docs/guides/connect-models#save-sources-in-desktop) and keep Docker running.
2. Open **Settings**, scroll to **Local engine**, and click **Start local engine**.
3. Wait for **Running**. Return to your pipeline, click **Run**, check its inputs, then **Start run**.

<DocScreenshot src="/images/beginner-07-engine.webp" width={1000} height={245} alt="Local engine settings showing Running and the Stop local engine button" caption="The engine is running. Stop local engine keeps its configuration and saved runs." />

Runs need their engine to stay running. The app-managed engine stops when you close Desktop and starts again when you reopen it after setup. A separately running engine keeps working when Desktop closes; reconnect to follow it.

## Open a saved result

<MermaidDiagram title="Reuse one plan for separate runs" chart={'flowchart LR\n  W[Workflow: saved plan] --> M[Monday run]\n  W --> F[Friday run]\n  M --> A[Monday artifacts]\n  F --> B[Friday artifacts]'} />

1. Click **Runs** in the sidebar.
2. Click the run with your pipeline's name and the start time you want.
3. Click **Outputs** to read its result. Open **Artifacts** for files if that pipeline creates them.

<DocScreenshot src="/images/beginner-10-result.webp" width={1218} height={410} alt="A completed My first pipeline run with its saved answer in Outputs" caption="A workflow is the reusable plan. This completed run is one execution of it." />

<details>
<summary>Local engine lifecycle and connection changes</summary>

Saved progress remains available after the engine restarts.

Model sources and MCP servers saved in Desktop apply to its managed local engine. Changes wait until every unfinished run completes, including paused runs, human waits, and scheduled retries. Existing runs keep their admitted configuration and credentials. Local settings do not replace a manually selected existing engine.

</details>
