---
title: How Knotra works
description: Understand the plan, the service doing the work, and the results you keep.
---

Desktop is your workspace. The engine is the service that does the work. Your team connects the engine to an AI model and any tools a workflow needs.

When you start a workflow in desktop, the engine performs its steps. Desktop shows progress and brings review requests to your Inbox.

## The words you will see

| Word | What it means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow or pipeline | A saved plan for doing a task | Prepare a customer announcement |
| Run | One attempt to carry out that plan | This week's announcement |
| Step | One part of the plan | Write the first draft |
| Profile | AI and tool settings prepared by your team | The settings your teammate tells you to choose |
| Artifact | A saved result file | The final announcement document |

## Follow an announcement through the app

AI drafts the announcement from the brief. A separate AI review compares it with the same brief. The process then pauses for a person.

You read the material in **Inbox** and give your response. After approval, AI revises the text. Knotra saves the final text and review record in **Artifacts**.

The review checks the supplied material. You still need to judge whether that material is correct and the final text is suitable to use.

## What happens if I close the app?

The engine continues working. Closing desktop stops your view of the work, not the work itself.

The engine keeps execution history and files across restarts when its saved data is retained. Your team is responsible for running the service and keeping its data safe.
