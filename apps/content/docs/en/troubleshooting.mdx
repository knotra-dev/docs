## The app says it is not connected

Open **Settings** and check the engine address supplied by your teammate. Choose **Connect engine**.

If it still fails, send your teammate the error shown on that page. They can check whether the service is running and whether you need an access token. You can still [try the guided demo](/docs/getting-started).

## Why is the work waiting for me?

Some workflows pause for a person's response. Open **Inbox** and choose the **Engine** tab for real work, or **Guided demo** for sample work.

Read the request before replying. In the announcement example, the next revision and final files are created after your approval.

## I pressed Run, but nothing seems to happen

The **Run** button opens a setup window. Choose the profile your teammate supplied, then choose **Start run**.

Check **Runs** for progress. If an error appears or no profiles are available, share that message with your teammate. Avoid starting the same task repeatedly while they check it.

## Where is my finished document?

Open **Runs** and check whether the run has completed. A review request may still be waiting in **Inbox**.

Then open **Artifacts** and choose the matching **Engine** or **Guided demo** tab. For the announcement example, look for `publication.md`. That file appears after review and revision.

If the run failed, share its name and the displayed error with your teammate.

## Does closing desktop stop the work?

No. Work continues on the engine. Reconnect to see its progress.

To stop an active engine run, open it in **Runs** and use **Request cancellation** when available.

## Do I need to install developer tools?

Ask your teammate to prepare the app and engine. You can use the guided demo and a prepared workflow without learning installation commands.

The person handling installation can follow [Technical setup](/docs/contributing).
