## Ready for real work?

Ask your teammate for the engine address, any access token, and a prepared profile. The engine does the work; the profile selects its AI and tool settings.

In **Settings**, enter the address, choose **Save address**, then **Connect engine**. Use the supplied token if required. Wait for **Connected**.

Runs may use a paid model. The person preparing your connection can follow [Technical setup](/docs/contributing).
